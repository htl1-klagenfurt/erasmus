Acta, non verba. Actions, not words.

Why we chose it. Everyone is talking about AI in schools. Teachers need more than talk: they need tools and methods they can use in class tomorrow. That is the promise of the name. Our Master Workshop in Split carries the motto “Acta, non verba” because it is about practice, not presentations.

The idea

Vocational teachers are the bridge between AI and the workplace.

True to its name, the project puts action first. It gives teachers at technical and vocational schools practical, tested ways to use AI in planning, teaching and assessment, and to help students build a confident professional identity next to it.

The work builds on the EU's newest frameworks for AI literacy and digital competence and is shaped by the needs of regional companies and SMEs.

Why ACTA · Survey, Dec 2025

Teachers see the potential. They lack the tools.

In December 2025 we asked 52 teachers at public VET schools in Austria, Slovenia and Croatia how they use AI. The answers shaped every module.

Objectives

Four goals, one cycle

From creating resources to training multipliers to lasting change across schools.

O1

Create needs-based resources

The ACTA Toolkit: eight practical modules that bring AI systematically into vocational classrooms.

O2

Teachers as multipliers

At least ten teachers trained in the Master Workshop carry the method back into their schools.

O3

Innovate and elevate

AI for personalised learning, engaging lessons and a smarter redesign of assessment and curricula.

O4

Scale and sustain

A community of practice inside the partnership and results shared with 30+ VET institutions in Europe.

The ACTA Toolkit

Eight modules, from first steps to course redesign

Each module becomes a 7–8 page booklet for EQF level 4 and 5 teaching: written in English, peer-reviewed by all partners, translated into German, Slovenian and Croatian, and piloted in real classrooms.

All booklets will be free to read and download as flipbooks on this website.

Master Workshop

Acta, non verba.

Three days of hands-on capacity building in Split for ten teachers from all partner schools. We test the modules together, refine the methodology and take it back to our classrooms.
